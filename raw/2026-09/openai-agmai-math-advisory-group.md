## What It Is

An independent body hosted at Princeton's Institute for Advanced Study (IAS) that
advises AI companies on their interactions with mathematical research and the
mathematical community. Announced via a guest post on Terence Tao's "What's New"
blog, written in the group's collective voice (not Tao's personal endorsement).

## Members (9 mathematicians)

François Charles, Camillo De Lellis, Timothy Gowers, Martin Hairer, Nikhil
Srivastava, Ulrike Tillmann, Ravi Vakil, Edward Witten, Melanie Matchett Wood.

## Origin

OpenAI approached some of these mathematicians about forming an external advisory
board specific to OpenAI. The mathematicians instead created an independent group
with broader scope — not OpenAI-controlled — to serve "the best interest of
mathematics and the mathematical community."

## Immediate Task

The group's stated current focus is coordinating how OpenAI releases "a large
number of significant results in mathematics" claimed to be produced by its
internal model — directly following the credit/priority controversy around
OpenAI's Navier-Stokes announcement (09-08) and the 25-Fields-medalist open
letter (09-12) criticizing AI labs' publication practices.
See: wiki/updates/2026-09-08-openai-navier-stokes-solved.md

## Reception

Comments on Tao's blog post show significant skepticism from the mathematical
community about collaborating with OpenAI at all. The Navier-Stokes dispute is
referenced mainly in comments, not in the group's own framing of its mandate.
