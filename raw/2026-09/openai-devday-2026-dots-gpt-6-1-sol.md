## Decrypt.co 요약 (WebFetch 정독, 2026-09-30)

### Dots: Always-On AI Agents

OpenAI introduced "dots," described as "remarkably capable, always-on agents" that operate
independently on their own cloud computers. These AI workers can:
- Access over 4,000 integrated applications
- Operate continuously, even when you're offline
- Learn from user feedback and preferences
- Connect via ChatGPT, voice calls, Slack, Teams, and upcoming SMS support

Availability: First dot included with Pro and Business Premium plans in eligible markets;
Free and Plus users excluded. Each dot runs on GPT-6 Astra (OpenAI's flagship model).

### GPT-6.1 Sol Model

"nearly matches GPT-6 Astra on agentic coding, computer use and professional work, at
one-fifth of Astra's token prices." Live across ChatGPT Work, Codex, API, and Education
tiers (Pro, Plus, Business, Enterprise).

### Premium Speed Tier

The Ultrafast option generates tokens up to 8x faster in Codex (300 tokens/second) at 6x
standard pricing, bundled with the new $500/month Pro 500 plan.

### ChatGPT Space

A collaborative workspace allowing humans and AI agents to co-edit documents and manage
shared projects together, with Pages designed specifically for human-AI collaboration.
