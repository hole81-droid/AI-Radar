# Anthropic and Accenture Partnership on Embedded Evaluation

Source: https://www.anthropic.com/news/accenture-embedded-evaluation
Published: 2026-09-18
Fetched: 2026-09-21 (WebFetch summary)

## What is Embedded Evaluation?
Embedded evaluation represents a new approach to AI safety oversight. Unlike
traditional external evaluators, embedded evaluators work inside AI companies with
employee-level access. This positioning allows them to "watch models take shape in
training, follow the decisions that govern how those models are built and deployed,
and speak directly to employees."

## Accenture's Role
Faculty, Accenture's specialist AI division, will lead the partnership and conduct:
- Model evaluation and red-teaming
- Alignment assessments
- Safeguard testing

The firm brings enterprise deployment experience, which informs their safety
methodology.

## Investment and Timeline
Both Anthropic and Accenture expect to invest at least $1 billion each over the next
five years to build capacity in this area.

## Connection to "We Must Pace the Frontier"
This partnership directly fulfills a commitment made in CEO Dario Amodei's essay
titled "We Must Pace the Frontier" to embed independent evaluators within Anthropic's
operations (see 2026-09-12 slowdown plan).

## Funding and Future Direction
Currently, Anthropic funds Accenture's work directly. The company is also discussing
embedded evaluation pilots with nonprofit evaluators like METR using their own
funding. Long-term, Anthropic advocates for pooled or government funding sources, as
previously outlined in their Advanced AI Framework. The partnership is non-exclusive —
Anthropic will work with other evaluators to be announced in coming weeks, and
Accenture will work with other AI developers in similar capacities.
