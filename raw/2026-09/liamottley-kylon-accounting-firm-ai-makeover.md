## What Happened

Liam Ottley (AI agency/business YouTuber) travels to Marbella to help Josh Thomas,
founder of Thomas & Co — described as "one of the fastest growing accountancy firms
in the UK" — fix its most tedious recurring task.

## The Problem

25 of Thomas & Co's accountants each spend more than half of every working week
(20+ hours/week per person) manually filling in "working papers" (accounting
documentation), by hand.

## The Build

- Tool: Kylon (Liam Ottley's own no-code AI agent/operating-system platform,
  promoted in the video — first month free via referral link).
- Constraint: Ottley states he has never worked a day of accounting in his life.
- By law, a human must still sign off on every number — the system cannot
  auto-submit financial figures without human review.
- Build time: 4 days.
- Verification: they ran the first real working paper through the system at the
  end of the 4 days to check whether it actually gave the 25 accountants their
  week back.

## Caveats (for benchmark honesty)

- This is a sponsored/promotional "AI Makeover" series episode for Ottley's own
  product (Kylon) — self-reported, not independently audited.
- No specific cost or underlying model (Claude/GPT/etc.) is disclosed in the
  description — Kylon is a no-code platform, the underlying LLM is unstated.
- Outcome numbers (time saved) are stated as the goal/problem framing ("won back
  their week") rather than a rigorously measured post-launch result in the
  description text.
