# Amazon Blocks Meta's Muse AI Shopping Agent

Source: WebSearch aggregation (Bloomberg, GeekWire, The Register, TechCrunch, Forbes, PYMNTS)
Blocked: starting Sunday night, on/around 2026-09-20~21
Fetched: 2026-09-22

## What Happened

Amazon began blocking Meta's new personal AI agent "Muse" (introduced earlier
in September 2026, designed to handle tasks like shopping and appointment
booking) from operating on Amazon.com, after Meta declined Amazon's request to
remove/adjust the bot.

Users attempting Amazon purchases through Muse saw a pop-up: "Continued access
by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon's Conditions of Use."

## Amazon's Stated Reasons

- Muse did not identify itself or disclose its intent to shop on Amazon's
  platform (no bot disclosure/user-agent transparency).
- Concerns about how Muse handles user login/session credentials (data
  security) — which Meta disputes.
- Amazon's general policy prohibits automated tools from shopping its site
  without authorization.

## Pattern — Not an Isolated Incident

This is the latest move in roughly a year of Amazon actions against
third-party AI shopping agents:
- Amazon sued Perplexity over its Comet browser's shopping-agent behavior.
- Amazon has separately moved to block shopping agents built by Google and
  OpenAI from its site.

## Why It Matters

As AI agents increasingly act as the "front door" for e-commerce (booking,
comparison shopping, purchasing), platform owners like Amazon are asserting
that they — not agent vendors — control who is allowed to transact
autonomously on their sites, citing data security and bot-disclosure norms.
This is an emerging competitive battleground: agent vendors (Meta, Google,
OpenAI, Perplexity) vs. platform owners (Amazon) over who mediates the
"last mile" of online commerce.
